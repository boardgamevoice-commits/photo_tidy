Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2025

In short: PhotoTidy completely respects your privacy. We do not collect, upload, or share any of your personal information or photos. All data remains on your device.

1. Introduction

Thank you for using PhotoTidy (hereinafter referred to as "this app" or "we"). We understand the importance of your privacy, so we have created this privacy policy to explain how we handle information related to your use of this app.

This privacy policy applies to the PhotoTidy iOS application. By using this app, you agree to the terms of this privacy policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

2.1 Information We Do Not Collect

PhotoTidy is a completely localized application. We do not:

  • Collect your personal identification information (such as name, email address, phone number, etc.)
  • Upload or transmit your photos, videos, or any media files
  • Track your location information
  • Collect your usage data or behavioral analytics
  • Access your contacts or other personal data
  • Use cookies or other tracking technologies

2.2 Locally Stored Data

This app stores the following data locally on your device, and this data does not leave your device:

  • App preferences: Such as language selection, interface theme, and other personalized settings
  • Session temporary data: Filter conditions and browsing progress temporarily cached during photo organization sessions

This data is only used to improve your user experience and is completely stored on your device.

3. Photo Library Access Permission

This app needs access to your photo library to provide photo organization functionality. We use the standard PhotoKit framework provided by iOS to access your photos.

Important Notes:

  • Photo access permission is completely under your control, and you can revoke this permission at any time in iOS settings
  • We only access photos you choose to view while the app is running
  • All photo processing (such as preview, filtering, deletion) is done locally on your device
  • We do not copy, upload, or transmit your photos in any way

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Advertising Services

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect certain information for ad delivery and analytics. This information may include:

  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version)
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • IP address
  • Ad interaction data

You can limit ad tracking through iOS settings. For more information about how Google uses data, please visit the Google Privacy Policy.

4.2 In-App Purchases

This app offers in-app purchases to remove advertisements. These transactions are handled by Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store your payment information.

5. Data Security

Since all photos and personal data remain on your device, data security primarily depends on your device's security measures (such as passwords, Face ID, Touch ID, etc.). We recommend that you:

  • Set a strong password for your device
  • Enable device encryption
  • Regularly backup your device data
  • Keep your iOS system updated to the latest version

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since we do not collect any personal information, the privacy protection for children using this app is the same as for adults.

7. Your Rights

Since we do not collect your personal information, there is no server-side data that needs to be deleted or modified. You can:

  • Revoke photo access permission at any time in iOS settings
  • Clear all locally stored app data by deleting the app
  • Reset app preferences in app settings

8. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and significant changes will be notified through in-app notifications. We recommend that you regularly review this policy to stay informed of the latest information.

Continued use of this app indicates your acceptance of changes to the privacy policy.
