About the App

PhotoTidy is a photo organization tool designed specifically for iOS. Through an intuitive card-based interface, you can quickly browse and manage your photo library, delete unwanted photos and videos, and free up device storage space.

The app completely respects your privacy. All photos and data remain on your device, and we do not collect, upload, or share any personal information.
