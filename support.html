User Guide & Frequently Asked Questions

User Guide

Quick Start

  1. Grant Permission: When launching the app for the first time, please grant photo library access permission
  2. Set Filter Conditions: Choose the date range and type of photos to organize
  3. Start Organizing: Quickly decide to keep or delete by swiping cards left or right
  4. Confirm Deletion: After the session ends, confirm the photos to be deleted

Gesture Operations

  • Swipe Right: Keep the current photo
  • Swipe Left: Mark the current photo for deletion
  • Tap Photo: View full-size image
  • Pinch to Zoom: Zoom in to view details in full-size mode
  • Tap Play Button: Play video or Live Photo

Filter Options

  • Date Range: Select photos from specific time periods
  • Media Type: Choose photos, videos, or Live Photos
  • Batch Mode: Quickly browse and delete similar photos

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can deleted photos be recovered?

A: Yes. Deleted photos will first go to the "Recently Deleted" folder in iOS Photos, where they will be kept for 30 days. During this time, you can recover accidentally deleted photos at any time.

Q: Will the app upload my photos?

A: Absolutely not. PhotoTidy runs completely locally on your device and will not upload, transmit, or share any of your photos or personal information.

Q: Why is photo access permission needed?

A: The app needs access to your photo library to display and manage photos. You can choose to grant "Full Access" or "Selected Photos" permission. We recommend granting full access for the best experience.

Q: How to remove ads?

A: You can purchase the "Remove Ads" feature through in-app purchase. This is a one-time payment, and all ads will be permanently removed after purchase.

Q: Does the app support iCloud Photo Library?

A: Yes. If you have enabled iCloud Photo Library, the app can access all synced photos and videos. Please note that deleting photos will also delete them from iCloud.

Q: Will progress in organization sessions be saved?

A: Session progress is temporarily saved. If you exit midway, you can choose to continue the previous session or start a new session when you launch the app next time.

Q: Does it support iPad?

A: Currently, the app is mainly optimized for iPhone, but it can also run on iPad. We plan to provide better iPad support in future versions.

Q: How to change the app language?

A: The app will automatically follow your device's system language settings. Currently supports Chinese and English.

System Requirements

  • iOS Version: iOS 14.0 or higher
  • Device: iPhone, iPad, and iPod touch
  • Storage: Approximately 20 MB
  • Permission: Photo library access permission

Tips & Tricks

  • Regularly organizing photos can save device storage space and improve system performance
  • We recommend starting with the oldest photos first, as it's easier to identify unwanted photos
  • Before organizing, you can add important photos to albums to avoid accidental deletion
  • Use the app in good lighting to better judge photo quality
  • Use pinch-to-zoom to carefully examine photo details
